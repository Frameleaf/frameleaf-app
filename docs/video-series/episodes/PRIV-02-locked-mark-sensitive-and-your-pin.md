## Voice-over (clean)

Locked keeps photos and videos private without moving them anywhere. It starts with a six-digit PIN.

Open Settings, then Access & security, then Locked folder PIN. Choose Create PIN, enter six digits and confirm them. If you ever forget it, Forgot your PIN resets it with your account password.

[pause]

Select what you want to keep private and choose Mark Sensitive in the selection bar. The items leave the timeline at once. Locking never needs your PIN; seeing them again does.

Nothing moves. A lock is a note on the item, so it keeps its albums, stack, favorites, tags and its place in time. Stacks and Live Photos lock as a whole.

The viewer's More menu has Mark Sensitive too, and anything you upload while in Locked stays locked.

[pause]

To see locked items, choose Locked in the top bar. Unlock Locked content asks for your PIN and checks it as soon as the sixth digit is in. The control then reads Revealed.

This lasts for the session only. Leave the tab or wait an hour, and everything hides again. Choose the control again to hide it right away.

[pause]

Locked in the sidebar lists every locked item, newest first. Filter by the reason: Marked by you, Detected by sensitive-content detection, or Moved from old Locked folder after an upgrade. Each tile's shield says Sensitive or Locked.

From here you can still add items to albums, download them, change their date or location, or delete them permanently.

To unlock, select items and choose Unmark Sensitive. Each goes back exactly where it was, and detection will not lock it again.

[pause]

While locked, an item is left out of the timeline, albums, search, the map, memories, people and downloads. Partners, album and space members and shared links never see it, whatever their own session.

A locked photo is never used as a cover or a featured photo. Background work such as thumbnails and backups still includes it, so nothing is skipped while it is hidden.

Next up: Detect sensitive content automatically.

## Production notes

- Sensitive examples are landscapes and documents only (STYLE-GUIDE.md section 6): Through the forest, Cabin at dusk and two plain scanned pages with no personal data. Nothing suggestive is ever shown. Never show PIN digits; the cells show dots.
- Labels verified in the build: Settings area "Access & security", group "Locked content", section "Locked folder PIN" ("Protect your Locked photos and videos"), status "Set a PIN before opening protected photos." / "A six-digit PIN protects your locked content.", buttons "Create PIN" / "Change PIN", "Clear PIN", "Forgot your PIN?" (dialog "Reset forgotten PIN", "Account password"), dialog fields "New six-digit PIN" and "Confirm PIN", toast "PIN created."; selection bar "Mark Sensitive" / "Unmark Sensitive" (Unmark Sensitive has Undo, Mark Sensitive does not); top bar control text "Locked" / "Revealed" with tooltips "Unlock Locked content" / "Hide Locked content" and a chevron that opens Locked; dialog "Unlock Locked content", "Reveal photos hidden by your rules and open your Locked collection.", "Six-digit PIN", "Content hides when you leave this tab or after one hour.", "PIN settings", "Cancel", "Unlock"; sidebar "Locked"; filter "Show locked items" with "All", "Moved from old Locked folder", "Marked", "Detected"; tile badges "Sensitive" / "Locked". The account menu shows "Locked content" with "Revealed for this session" / "Hidden until you unlock".
- The PIN cells submit on the sixth digit ("The PIN is checked as soon as all six are entered."); the Unlock button is there too. If password sign-in is off, "Forgot your PIN?" says to ask the administrator (OPS-12 covers the admin reset).
- locked.md is the source for every rule narrated: a lock is metadata; stacks and Live Photos lock whole; locking needs no PIN, unlocking does; the reasons and badges; actions in Locked; Unmark Sensitive records the review as safe so detection never relocks; left out of timeline, albums, search, map, memories, people, pets and downloads; partners, album and space members and shared links never see it; never a cover, featured photo or face thumbnail source; server jobs always process it. Your own devices still sync locked items, marked locked (not narrated).
- The "Moved from old Locked folder" reason exists only on libraries upgraded from the previous Locked folder; show it from an upgraded test library or keep the ZOOM on the chip names only. Items with that reason stay in Locked only after unlocking, rather than reappearing in the timeline.
- "Anything you upload while in Locked stays locked": uploads started from the Locked area are locked (locked.md "an upload into Locked"; top bar upload defaults).
- The older "Move to Locked folder" wording is gone from the web build; do not capture it.
- Locking is privacy, not encryption; the VO never says encrypted.
- Outro card: "Next: PRIV-03 · Detect sensitive content automatically".
