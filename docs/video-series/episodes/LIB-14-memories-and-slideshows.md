## Voice-over (clean)

Memories brings back days worth revisiting. If Memories is not in your sidebar, open Account Settings, then Features, and turn on Show Memories in the sidebar.

The page groups them into Today, Upcoming for the next two weeks, and Earlier for trips and highlights. Year in review gathers a year's highlights, and a birthday memory arrives for any person or pet whose date of birth you set.

Choose a memory to play it. Photos drift with a slow pan and zoom, and a line at the bottom names the place and day. Space pauses, and the arrow keys step through.

Remove from memory drops a photo you would rather not see, with Undo if you change your mind. Open the gallery and choose Reorder to move photos earlier or later, or rename the memory.

On the Memories page, each card's menu can hide a memory, or show less of a person, a date or a kind of memory. Hidden memories, at the bottom, restores anything you hid.

Export this memory builds a zip of the original files on the server. You can leave the page while it works, then choose Download export. Only your own items go in, and nothing archived, locked or in the trash.

For a slideshow, open a photo and press S, or choose Slideshow above an album. Slideshow settings set the photo duration, fit, captions and order, and the transition: None, Fade, Slide, Ken Burns, or Memories, which adds title cards.

Repeat collection loops it, and videos always play to their end. These settings are kept in this browser.

To show photos on a TV, turn on Enable Chromecast under Features. It is experimental and off by default, because it loads scripts from Google. It needs your server on a public HTTPS address, and the Cast button then appears in the viewer.

Play a memory, keep what matters, and share it on the big screen.

Next up: Your account and preferences.

## Production notes

- Interface vs docs: no user doc covers the Memories page, the player, export or slideshow settings (FAQ.mdx has no memories or slideshow entries), so every label here comes from the current interface. casting.md gives the path "Account Settings > Features > Cast > Google Cast"; the current switch is Features → "Enable Chromecast" ("Offer casting when a compatible receiver is available."). An administrator can turn casting off per account ("Allow casting"), and the switch then reads "Turned off by your administrator". Flag casting.md and the FAQ.
- casting.md: casting is "considered experimental", web only (mobile support is planned, so it is not mentioned), off by default because the page loads Google's scripts, and needs the server reached over HTTPS with a public DNS record Google's resolvers can see, plus Cast-compatible video formats. The Cast framework loads when the app starts; reload the page after turning it on. Blur the TV's model name and the network name in the picker.
- Memories is off in the sidebar by default ("Show Memories in the sidebar" starts off). Year in review is generated seven days into the new year and needs at least 20 items that year; a birthday memory needs a date of birth on a named person or pet and at least three photos. Emma's age is sample data.
- Many player controls are icon-only: Remove from memory, Export this memory, Download export, Rename memory, Share memory, Favorite item. CALLOUTs name them. The soundtrack is the memory's own video sound and starts muted. Keys: Space, M (sound), G (gallery), arrows, Esc.
- "Export this memory" makes a zip of originals, not a video. It runs as a server job, re-checks download permission, takes up to 2,000 items, is kept for 24 hours, and downloads as "{title}.zip". The player also shows "Make a movie in Studio"; Studio episodes are on HOLD, so that button is not narrated or clicked.
- Under Reduce Motion the player and the Ken Burns transition fall back to a crossfade; capture with motion allowed.
- Slideshow settings are stored in this browser only. The Memories transition shows a title card once per run, then the place and day as a lower third.
- Beat 6's hidden memory is restored after capture.
