## Voice-over (clean)

Every photo with a location lands on the map. Open Map, under Explore. Nearby photos gather into clusters, and the count at the top says how many items are in view.

Hover over a cluster to see its size, and click to zoom in until it splits. Single photos show as small thumbnails. Click one to open it, and the viewer steps through everything in view.

Move the map, and Search this area appears. It opens your library limited to that part of the map, so you can select, share or add to an album. The cross beside Map area clears it.

Show list opens In view, a list of everything on screen with its date and place. The target button beside a row centres the map on it.

Map settings choose what the map shows. Pick a date range, and under Include, turn Partner items and Shared spaces on or off to add or remove photos that others share with you. Archived items and Only favorites are there too.

Places lists where your photos were taken, grouped by country, then by region, then by city. Find a place searches the names. Click a city to see its photos, or Show on map to jump there.

Place names come from a geographic database kept on your own server. If one is wrong, open the photo and use Edit location. A name you type there is kept.

The map itself is drawn from an online map service. An administrator can change its look, or its provider, under Server and updates, then Maps and geography. Paste a style address into Light style and Dark style, and save.

Zoom into clusters, search an area to act on it, and browse by Places.

Next up: Tags and Folders.

## Production notes

- Interface vs docs: custom-map-styles.md gives the path "Administration --> Settings --> Map & GPS Settings → Map Settings" with fields "Light Style" / "Dark Style". The current path is Administration → Your server → Server & updates → Maps & geography → group "Map", with "Enable map features", "Light style" and "Dark style" (both disabled while the map is off), saved with the settings bar's "Save changes". No doc covers clusters, Search this area, In view, Map settings or the Places page; labels come from the interface. Flag both docs.
- Reverse geocoding runs against GeoNames data loaded into the server's own database (reverse-geocoding.md), which is what beat 8 narrates. Map tiles are different: the admin page states "The map feature relies on an external tile service (…)". Crop or blur that subtitle in beat 9, because it names the upstream tile domain; the VO says only "an online map service". The custom-style guide's walkthrough of a third-party style editor is not shown and no provider is named.
- Map settings are saved per browser. Defaults: Shared spaces and Partner items on, everything else off, All time. "Shared spaces" controls albums and spaces shared with you. The legend labels "1 · 2–9 · 10+" are fixed text.
- The list, zoom, Show all items and settings tools are icon-only; CALLOUTs name them ("Show list", "Map settings"). "Search this area" appears only after you move the view yourself.
- If an administrator turns the map off, the Map page reads "The map is turned off" with "Open Places"; Places keeps working without map tiles (its state cards use a drawn dot map).
- An album header also has a "Map" action that opens the map limited to that album; not shown.
- reverse-geocoding.md says coordinates "can be moved but not removed"; the current Edit location dialog has "Remove location" (see LIB-02). Not used here.
- Counts in beats 2 to 7 are sample values from the prepared library; keep them consistent across the cut.
