## Voice-over (clean)

A preservation package is an independent copy of your originals, with what your library knows about them: dates, places, descriptions, albums, people, tags and edit recipes. It is in Import and protection, under Originals and preservation.

It is not a backup of the server. Accounts, sharing, settings, pets, memories and Studio projects are not included, so keep your database backups as well.

Choose Preview preservation manifest. Under What to preserve, pick the whole library, favorites, a range of dates or chosen albums. Only your own items are ever included. Name the package and choose Continue.

Verify counts and measures the selection, and checks that it fits in one package, up to one hundred thousand items, and in the free space on the server. Nothing is written yet.

Manifest shows, category by category, what a restoration would bring back. Choose Start export.

The export is a background job in Activity, and closing the page does not stop it. Each original is copied and checked against its recorded checksum; your library's files are never changed.

A new package reads Not verified. Verify files reads it back and checks every original and sidecar against its checksum. Then it reads Verified.

[pause]

Download package saves it as one ZIP. A finished download is not a verified copy, so verify the copy you keep elsewhere as well.

To bring a package back, choose Restore from a package. Pick a saved package, upload a ZIP, or, as an administrator, a path on the server. Choose what wins where the package and your library disagree.

Check package verifies every file and compares each original with your library, without writing anything. Review then sorts originals into new, already in your library, and in your trash.

For photos you already have, each difference is listed; choose Keep library or Use package. Choose Restore. Existing originals are never replaced or copied twice, and nothing is removed.

[pause]

## Production notes

- Path: preservation.md says "Settings → Originals & preservation" (administrators also under Import & protection) and "Utilities → Preservation verification". In the current build every account finds "Originals & preservation" inside the Import & protection area, and no Utilities entry exists. Flag the doc.
- The sample library (153,792 items) exceeds the 100,000-item package limit, so the capture preserves two albums; choosing Whole library shows "One package holds up to 100,000 items. Narrow the selection, for example by year or album."
- The interface also offers "Search" and "Selected items" under What to preserve (not in the doc); they are in shot but not narrated. "Include Locked items" works only from an unlocked session, and such a package can only be downloaded from an unlocked session.
- Package states in the build: "Writing", "Not verified", "Verifying", "Verified", "Incomplete", "Problems found", "Paused", "Failed". An item that fails is tried once more automatically; the rest are listed in "Item report" and can be retried with "Retry failed items". A package with uncopied items reads "Incomplete".
- Verify files (doc): reads the manifest, then every original and sidecar against its SHA-256, and reports each as matching, missing or changed, plus unexpected files; a package this server wrote is also compared with the server's own record. To verify an off-server copy, check it through Restore from a package with Upload or, for an administrator, Path on server (the Check step verifies every file and writes nothing). An uploaded package is kept for three days.
- Download: one ZIP named `name.frameleaf-preservation.zip`; "Download manifest" saves the manifest alone.
- Restore rules (doc): originals matched by checksum, never replaced or copied twice; an original in your trash is not added again ("Restore them from the trash first, then restore again."); nothing is removed, favorites and Locked records are only added, Locked items come back Locked; a stopped restore carries on without touching items already restored; albums, collections and people are found again rather than duplicated. Generated descriptions and captions come back as provenance only, never as your own text.
- Checksums detect damage, not deliberate rewriting; restore only packages from a source you trust.
- Sizes, counts and dates are sample data.
