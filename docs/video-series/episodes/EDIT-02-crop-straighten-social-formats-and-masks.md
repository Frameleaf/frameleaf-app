## Voice-over (clean)

Open a photo in Quick edit and choose Crop. The panel is called Crop and straighten, and nothing you do here cuts anything from the original file.

Drag a corner or an edge to reshape the crop. Drag inside it to move it. With the crop focused, the arrow keys nudge it, and Shift moves it further.

Aspect locks the shape. Free draws any rectangle, Original keeps the photo's own ratio, Square is square, and fixed ratios run from 16:9 to 4:5.

Straighten levels a tilted horizon a degree at a time. Rotate left and Rotate right turn the photo a quarter turn. Flip horizontal and Flip vertical mirror it.

Social formats live in Presets. Portrait 4:5 is for a feed, Shorts 9:16 is vertical and Wide 16:9 is landscape. Each sets the crop and aspect in one click. Fine-tune the framing in Crop.

Back in Crop, Reset crop returns the full photo at any time.

Masks adjust part of a photo instead of all of it. Open Masks and choose Radial or Linear.

A radial mask is an oval you place with its handles. Light and color changes then apply only inside it. Feather softens the edge, Amount scales the effect, and Invert applies it outside instead.

A linear mask is a gradient. Drag Gradient start and Gradient end across the sky, then lower Exposure to hold the clouds.

Each mask is listed with its number and kind. A photo can have up to eight masks. Turn one off to compare, or choose Remove all masks to start again.

Save version keeps the crop and the masks together as one edit. Applying a preset never changes the crop, so it is safe to add a Look next.

Crop and straighten for the frame, Social formats for a post, and masks for a part of the picture. The original is untouched in every case.

[pause] Next up: Looks, presets and versions.

## Production notes

- Prerequisites: Taylor signed in; Ridge trail and Emma at the lake processed. No server settings are involved.
- Aspect: the UI offers Free, Original, Square and the fixed ratios 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:2 and 4:5 (web/src/lib/frameleaf/develop.ts `ASPECTS`). The inventory lists only Free, Original and Square; the extra ratios are shown but the VO names only the ends of the range.
- Social formats: they sit in the Presets tool, under the looks strip and the strength slider, not in Crop. The UI has three formats, Portrait 4:5, Shorts 9:16 and Wide 16:9, each with a small note (Feed, Vertical, Landscape). The inventory reads as if Feed, Vertical and Landscape were three further formats; they are the notes under the three buttons. Do not add extra buttons in the capture.
- Flip: the on-screen labels are "Flip horizontal" and "Flip vertical" (i18n `editor_flip_horizontal`, `editor_flip_vertical`); the inventory shortens this to "Flip".
- Straighten shows a "{degrees} degrees" readout. The exact slider range is not documented; keep the demo within a few degrees.
- Masks: the help line reads "Adjust part of the photo. Light and color changes apply inside the mask, or outside it when inverted." Each mask is titled "Mask {number} · {kind}" and carries Feather, Invert and Amount plus the Light and Color sliders. Linear handles are "Gradient start" and "Gradient end"; radial handles are "Mask width" and "Mask height". The limit is 8 masks (`MAX_MASKS` in web/src/lib/frameleaf/photo-tools.ts) and the panel shows "A photo can have up to 8 masks." when reached; the VO says "eight".
- The preset help line quoted in beat 12 is "A preset keeps your light, color, effects, detail, look and masks. The crop never changes when you apply one."
- Correction (checked against the current build, web/src/lib/components/frameleaf/editor/QuickEditor.svelte): Social formats are part of the Presets panel; the Crop and straighten panel holds only the crop frame, Aspect, Straighten, Orientation and Reset crop. Beats 6 and 7 now go Crop → Presets for the formats, then back to Crop for Reset crop. The beat 12 line "Applying a preset never changes the crop" is about looks and saved presets; the Social formats buttons in the same panel do change the crop, as beat 6 says.
- Doc mismatch: docs/docs/features/editing.mdx lists only Cropping, Rotation and Mirroring as supported edits; masks, straighten and social formats are described from the UI strings and the inventory.
