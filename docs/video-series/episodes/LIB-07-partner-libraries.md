## Voice-over (clean)

A partner library shares your whole library with one other person on your server. It is one-way. To see their photos as well, they share theirs with you.

Open Account Settings, then People and sharing. Under Partner libraries, choose Add partner and pick an account. Review partner access explains what they will see: Locked media stays restricted, and nothing asks them to share back. Choose Confirm.

Your partner sees every photo and video that is not archived or in the trash, with its metadata. They do not see your favorites, your people and face data, or your existing albums.

When Jamie shares back, Jamie's library appears in your sidebar under Shared spaces, and on the Shared spaces page under Partners.

It opens like your own library, with the same grid and viewer. The originals stay in Jamie's account, so changes are left to Jamie, but you can still download what you need.

Turn on Show in timeline, and Jamie's photos join your main timeline. Turn it off, and they stay in Jamie's library. If you both keep the same photo, you may see it twice, because duplicates are found per person.

Where your photos were taken is your choice. Because you share with Jamie too, Share my location with this partner appears. Turn it off, and Jamie no longer sees the places on the photos you share. Frameleaf reminds you that earlier locations may already have been seen.

To hand over the files themselves, select photos and choose Send a copy. Your device's share sheet opens with the originals, and no link or share is created. Locked and trashed items are left out, and so are photos from a partner who keeps locations private.

Stop sharing, in Settings, ends Jamie's access to your library. Jamie's originals, and anything Jamie shares with you, stay as they are.

Next up: The search palette.

## Production notes

- Interface vs docs: partner-sharing.md still describes the older screens ("Account Settings > Partner Sharing > Show in timeline", partners reached "via the Sharing page", removal "click on the X button"). The current interface is Account Settings → People & sharing → section "Partners & recipient groups" → "Partner libraries" with "Add partner", the dialog "Review partner access" (Account, Cancel, Confirm), per-partner rows ("Can see your library" / "You are not sharing with this account", "Shares with you" / "Does not share with you"), the toggles "Show shared photos in my timeline" and "Share my location with this partner", and a "Stop sharing" button. Flag partner-sharing.md for an update.
- The location toggle and "Send a copy…" are not in partner-sharing.md; both are narrated from the current interface. The location toggle only appears for a partner you also share with; otherwise the header reads "You are not sharing your library with Jamie, so there are no locations of yours to share."
- The doc line "Access to all non-archived and trashed photos and videos" is read as "not archived and not in the trash" (the partner page shows timeline items only). The doc's exclusions (favorites, people and facial recognition data, existing albums) and the duplicate note are narrated in beats 4 and 7.
- Actions that change an item (favorite, archive, album changes, tags and so on) skip items owned by someone else, with the reason "Owned by someone else"; only Download is exempt. Beat 6 therefore shows Download only. The partner page's code comment still lists share link and add to album; do not show them on partner items until that is confirmed.
- The partner header also has a button "Stop sharing your photos with this user", which removes your own sharing towards that partner. Beat 10 uses the Settings "Stop sharing" button instead so the direction is clear on screen.
- "Send a copy…" appears only in browsers whose share sheet accepts files; capture on one that does. It takes up to 50 items and 500 MB ("Choose up to 50 items to send a copy."). Skipped items are reported by toasts: "1 item in Locked or the trash was not sent." and "1 item from a partner who keeps locations private was not sent." Blur the operating system's app icons in the share sheet.
- Toast texts: "Partner added.", "Jamie’s photos now appear in your timeline.", "Jamie’s photos stay in their own library.", "Locations are now hidden from Jamie.", "Jamie can see where your shared photos were taken.", "Stopped sharing your library."
- The doc's mobile toggle (from the partner's view in the mobile app) is not shown; this episode is web only.
- Beat 10's dialog is cancelled; no sharing is removed during capture.
