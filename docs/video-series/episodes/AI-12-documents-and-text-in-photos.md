## Voice-over (clean)

Documents gathers every photo with readable text: receipts, signs, letters and screenshots. Open it from the Explore section of the sidebar.

Search text in your photos finds them by their words, or by your own corrections. Search LAKE AGNES and the trailhead sign comes straight up.

Text is read automatically on your own hardware, so it can contain mistakes. You can fix them.

[pause]

Open the photo. The information card ends with Text in this photo. Show text regions draws a box around each line, right on the photo.

Select text highlights it all, and Copy text puts it on your clipboard.

[pause]

As the owner, you can review each line. Open Review lines and choose Correct on a misread line. Your correction is stored beside what was read, never over it, and search finds both.

Dismiss hides a line that is only noise, and Restore brings it back. Hover a line, or choose Show where, to see where it sits on the photo.

Read text again sends the photo back through text recognition, on the destination chosen for it in Settings. With none chosen, it tells you instead of sending the photo anywhere.

People you share the photo with read it with your corrections and without the lines you dismissed. Only you see the review. A locked photo's text is listed only while your session is unlocked.

[pause]

For receipts, an administrator can turn on Suggest receipt and document fields, in the OCR settings under Search & intelligence. It is off by default.

Suggested details then offers a date, a total, a reference, an email address and a phone number from the text. Each is only what the text says, never a verified record. Confirm it, pick one of the other readings, correct it or dismiss it.

Next up: Privacy by design.

- "Read text again" states in the build: "Text recognition is switched off." / "Text recognition has no processing destination yet. Choose one under Settings, Processing destinations." (administrators) / "…; ask your administrator." (members). The docs' "With no destination chosen, or with text recognition switched off, it says so instead of sending the photo anywhere" is what the VO paraphrases.
- "On your own hardware": text recognition always stays on your own workers (this server or a home-network worker) and never runs on Frameleaf Cloud (workers-and-endpoints.md).
- Beat 10: documents.md says people you share with read the photo "with your corrections applied and without the lines you dismissed. Only you see your review." A locked photo's text "is listed and readable only while your session is unlocked". Keep any locked example a document photo.
- Day and month order in a suggested date is never guessed, and a well-read "12.50" can still be the wrong total (docs); the VO's "only what the text says, never a verified record" covers this.
- The receipt is a seeded document photo (café name, total, date, reference only); no personal names, card numbers or addresses. Blur anything that looks like a card number.
- Conflicts: a change made against an older version of the text is refused and the panel reloads; not narrated.
- The docs place the toggle "in the OCR section of the machine learning settings"; in the build it is Settings → Search & intelligence, group "OCR".
- Outro card: "Next: PRIV-01 · Privacy by design" (first episode of the next series in the index).
