## Voice-over (clean)

Frameleaf's pages share one frame: the top bar, the sidebar on the left, and your photos in the middle. Here's what lives where.

The switcher in the top bar holds three workspaces. Library is your photos and videos. Studio holds your projects and has its own series. Activity lists uploads, downloads and background jobs while they run.

In the middle sits Search your library. Press slash, or Control K, then type what you're looking for. Start with a greater-than sign and it becomes the command palette: type a page, a setting or an action, and press Enter.

Upload offers files or a whole folder, and an album to add them to. The next episode walks through it.

Next to Upload: a job count that appears while work is running, the notifications bell, Locked, which hides or reveals your Locked photos with your PIN, the theme switch, and your account menu.

In the library, press the question mark to see every keyboard shortcut. Shortcuts pause while you type in a field.

[pause]

The sidebar starts with your library views: Library, Favorites, Recently added, Best Photos, Archive and Locked. Then come Explore, with People, Pets, Places, Map and more; Albums, with your collections and Shared links; Shared spaces; and Tools, with Workflows and Trash.

Click a heading to fold its section away; Frameleaf remembers that on this device. The arrows at the top shrink the sidebar to icons.

Three entries sit at the bottom. Library Care opens the checks and repairs that look after your files. Settings holds your own preferences and, for administrators, every server setting, grouped into areas you can search.

Support Frameleaf explains how the project is funded: Frameleaf is free to self-host, and a supporter key adds a badge that says thanks.

[pause]

Account Settings in your account menu opens Settings too. Your preferences holds your profile and appearance, and Access and security holds your password, Locked PIN, devices and API keys.

Personal settings change only your own account. Everything else in this series starts from one of these places.

The written guide is linked below.

## Production notes

- Labels verified in the build: switcher "Library", "Studio", "Activity" (navigation.ts); search field "Search your library" with the hint "Ctrl K" (or "⌘ K" on macOS); palette placeholder "Search photos, people, places… or > for commands"; "Command palette" with "Type a command or search for a page…" and groups "Pages", "Settings", "Actions"; Upload menu "Upload files", "Upload folder", "Add to", "Library only"; Locked control "Locked" / "Unlock Locked content"; theme tooltip "Switch to light theme"; "Keyboard shortcuts" dialog (General, Actions, "Done"); rail headings "Explore", "Albums", "Shared spaces", "Tools"; rail items "Recently added", "Best Photos", "All albums", "Shared links", "Workflows", "Trash"; footer "Library Care", "Settings", "Support Frameleaf"; "Collapse navigation" / "Expand navigation"; Settings groups "Command center", "Your library", "Your server", "Personal" and search "Find a setting…"; account menu "Account Settings"; areas "Your preferences" and "Access & security".
- Key caps: capture on the platform the producer prefers and show the matching hint (Ctrl K or ⌘ K) on the key cap; the VO says "Control K" in both cases. The palette can also be opened directly with Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + P; not narrated. `?` opens the shortcuts sheet from the library grid and the viewer.
- Studio: episodes EDIT-06 and EDIT-07 are HOLD, so do not open Studio here. The VO only names it and says it has its own series.
- Support Frameleaf: prices and plans belong to the CLOUD series (HOLD). Keep the plan cards, credit packs and any USD figures out of frame; hold on the heading and the intro paragraph.
- Docs vs interface: user-settings.md says user settings open from "the user icon on the top right corner" via "Account Settings" and describes a flat list (Your profile, Password, Locked folder PIN, API keys, Sign-in provider, Signed-in devices, Supporter status). In the current build "Account Settings" opens the Settings Command Center, where these live in the "Your preferences" and "Access & security" areas. The narration follows the build; flag user-settings.md for an update. README.md carries no interface tour; the switcher, rail order and footer come from `web/src/lib/frameleaf/navigation.ts`.
- Beat 10: the Library care area itself is covered in CARE-01; show it for two seconds only. Administrator areas (Overview, Library analytics, Users, Libraries, Frameleaf Cloud) appear in the Settings list because Taylor is an administrator; a non-admin sees only the areas that apply to their account.
- The activity count appears only while something runs; start a background job just before the capture. Keep Locked content hidden throughout; the PIN dialog in beat 6 is cancelled without entering a PIN.
- CTA card: `Guide: User Settings` (producer fills in the public docs URL).
