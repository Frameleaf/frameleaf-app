## Voice-over (clean)

Duplicate review is where you decide what happens to copies Frameleaf has found. Open Library Care, then Duplicate groups under Repair queues. It is also in Settings, under Utilities.

The Review queue lists every group and says whether it holds copies or burst frames. Show Needs attention hides groups you have already decided, and Find items searches by filename.

A group of two opens side by side. The suggested keeper is marked with its reasons, here Original format and Highest resolution. Where the copies differ lists what does not match, and Zoom both inspects the same spot in each.

That suggestion comes from Prefer original format, which is on by default. It picks the camera RAW, then HEIC, even when a re-saved JPG is bigger. Administrators find it in Search and intelligence, under Duplicate Detection.

Choose Keep suggested. The other copy moves to the trash, its albums, tags and favorite carry over to the keeper, and the next group opens. Undo takes back any recent decision.

If the photos are not really duplicates, choose Keep all and the group leaves the queue. Stack together keeps every copy, shown as one stack.

A burst is different: frames seconds apart are separate moments, so nothing is suggested. The contact sheet shows every frame. Pick your keepers and choose Keep selected, or Stack entire burst to keep them all.

Keys make it quick. A number keeps that copy, or picks frames on a contact sheet. K keeps the suggestion, A keeps all, S stacks, E keeps your selection, and the arrow keys move between groups.

To clear many groups at once, tick Select matching groups, check how many copies would move to the trash, and choose Keep suggested in the selected groups.

Videos are compared using several frames sampled from inside each clip, so black frames and title cards no longer cause false matches. Play both from the start to watch the copies side by side.

[pause]

Nothing is removed until you choose an action, and space is freed only when the trash is emptied.

[pause]

Next up: Missing originals: locate and relink.

## Production notes

- Path: Library care → Repair queues → "Duplicate groups" opens Settings → Utilities → Duplicate review (`/user-settings?area=utilities&section=duplicates`). The region is announced as "Fast duplicate review".
- Docs vs interface: duplicates-utility.md still describes the older "Review duplicates" utility with "Deduplicate All" and a pre-selection by file size, then EXIF count. The current tool is "Duplicate review" with "Keep suggested", "Keep all", "Stack together" and "Skip for now", bulk "Keep suggested in {n} groups", "Keep all copies" and "Stack groups", and the footnote "Keeper suggestions use resolution, format and original provenance." README.md gives the Prefer original format path as "Admin > System Settings > Machine Learning > Duplicate Detection"; in the current build it is Settings → Search & intelligence → Machine Learning Settings → Duplicate Detection. Narration follows the current interface; flag duplicates-utility.md for an update.
- Metadata merge (beat 6) is from duplicates-utility.md: when exactly one copy is kept, the keeper joins every album of the group, gains favorite and the highest rating, combined descriptions, the most restrictive visibility, tags, and the location when all located copies agree. Keeping several merges nothing. Undo puts back what the merge changed unless a newer edit replaced it.
- Burst groups: the server reads a group as a burst when every member is a photo with its own capture time and all fall within 20 seconds; bursts never get a suggestion ("Burst frames need a personal choice."). The contact sheet is also used for any group of more than two copies ("Review the whole group"), with "Keep {n} selected" instead of Keep suggested.
- Keyboard strings to reproduce exactly (shortcut panel): "1–9 Select keepers on this page" on a contact sheet or "1–9 Keep that copy" in a two-copy compare, "K Keep suggested", "A Keep all", "S Stack", "E Keep selected" (contact sheet only), "← → Previous / next group", "⌘ / Ctrl Z Undo". The VO covers both meanings of the number keys.
- Enhanced video duplicate detection (README.md): sampled internal frames compared with CLIP reduce false groups from black frames, title cards, intro screens, similar openings and different-resolution copies. Defaults in the current build: on, Video frame count 4, Minimum matching video frames 2, Video frame match distance 0.01. Detection runs as the "Photo duplicates" and "Video duplicates" queues.
- Keep the trash turned on for capture. With the trash off the footer reads "The trash is turned off: copies you do not keep are deleted permanently." and deciding opens "Delete the other copies permanently?"; do not show that dialog here.
- A group that includes another account's photo reads "This group includes a photo from another account. Only the owner of every photo in a group can decide what to keep."; a group with Locked members hidden in this session reads "Unlock to review". Neither is narrated.
- The Library care setting "Group near-duplicates for review" must be on; when it is off, the queue reads "Grouping is turned off in Library care settings".
- File sizes, counts and group names on screen are sample data.
