## Voice-over (clean)

Editing in Frameleaf never changes the original file. Every edit is a recipe, and you can go back to the original at any time. To begin, select a photo in the library and choose Quick edit.

The editor opens on Adjust. The other tools sit beside it: Crop, Masks, Presets and Restore. Versions keeps every edit you save.

Adjust has four groups. Light holds Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites and Blacks. Drag Exposure up to brighten the whole photo, then pull Highlights down to bring the sky back.

The histogram at the top follows the preview. When a tone is lost it says so, for example Highlights are clipping. Ease the slider back until the warning clears.

Color holds Temperature, Tint, Vibrance and Saturation. A small move on Temperature warms the evening light on the lake.

Effects holds Clarity, Dehaze, Vignette and Grain. Small moves go a long way here.

Detail holds Sharpen and Noise reduction. Double-click any slider to reset it. Reset all adjustments clears the whole panel.

To check your work, hold the backslash key, or Y, and the original shows for as long as you hold. Or turn on Split view before and after and drag the divider across the photo.

When the look is right, choose Copy adjustments. Open the next photo from the same evening in Quick edit and choose Paste adjustments. The whole recipe lands on Ridge trail in one click.

Choose Save version to keep the edit. Frameleaf renders an edited master from the original plus your recipe, and the status moves from Queued to Rendering to Rendered.

That is Adjust. Four groups of sliders, a histogram that warns you before you lose a tone, and a recipe you can copy to the next photo.

Versions lists the original and every saved edit. To show the original again, choose Make current on its row. The original file is never touched.

[pause] Next up: Crop, straighten, social formats and masks.

## Production notes

- Prerequisites: Taylor signed in; Moraine Lake and Ridge trail present and fully processed (thumbnails and previews generated). No server-side settings are needed for the photo quick editor; rendering the edited master runs on the server itself.
- Entry points: the selection bar's "Quick edit" is shown here. The viewer also has an "Edit" button (i18n `frameleaf_viewer_edit`), and tiles offer hover quick actions; either can be used if the capture flow prefers it. Say only "Quick edit" in the VO.
- Slider values in the visual column (+0.40 EV, −35, +8, +10, +6, +30) are capture guidance, not product limits. Exposure ranges −2 to +2 EV in steps of 0.05.
- The histogram label reads "Approximate" while the CSS preview is shown and "From render" once the server preview matches; either is fine on camera. The clipping labels are "Highlights are clipping" and "Shadows are clipping".
- Compare controls: the hold button's tooltip is "Hold to show the original (\ or Y)"; the split control is "Split view before and after" with a draggable "Before and after divider". "Compare with original" is a separate control in the Versions panel (used in EDIT-03).
- Copy adjustments and Paste adjustments live in the editor toolbar and again under "More actions". Toasts are "Adjustments copied" and "Adjustments pasted".
- Version statuses on screen: "Queued", "Rendering {progress}%", "Rendered", "Render failed", "Cancelled", "Saved, not rendered". The toast after saving is "Version 1 saved. Rendering the edited master…".
- Doc mismatch: docs/docs/features/editing.mdx still lists only Cropping, Rotation and Mirroring under "Supported Edits" and shows the previous edit interface. The current quick editor tools come from the inventory and the UI strings; the doc's sections on originals, edited masters and playback copies remain accurate and are the basis for beats 11 and 13.
- Correction (checked against the current build, web/src/lib/components/frameleaf/editor/QuickEditor.svelte): the top-bar "Revert" (tooltip "Revert the settings to the original") only resets the edit in progress to the original settings; it does not change which version is current. The original becomes current again through "Make current" on the Original row of the Versions panel (toast "The original is current again"), which is what beat 13 now shows. "Make current" appears only while a saved version is current; a version becomes current once it has rendered. Save version also closes the editor, so reopen Quick edit on Moraine Lake before beat 13 (beat 11 shows the reopen before the statuses).
- Do not show the "Restore" tool in use; it is covered by EDIT-05 (HOLD).
