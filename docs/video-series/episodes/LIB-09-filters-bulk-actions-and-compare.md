## Voice-over (clean)

Search finds photos. This episode is about what comes next: narrowing a view with filters, acting on many items at once, and picking the best shot with Compare.

Choose Filter above any grid, or pick a section from the menu beside it. Each filter you set becomes a chip, like People, or City: Banff. Click a chip to change it, or its cross to remove it.

To leave something out, switch a group from Any of to Exclude, and its chip reads Not, like Not Tags. Yes or No choices work the same way, so No under Favorites reads Not Favorite. Clear all removes every filter at once.

Select items by their check marks, and the selection bar appears. Select all takes in the whole view. The everyday actions sit right on the bar: favorite, add to an album, share, send a copy, download and delete.

More actions holds the rest, in groups. Organize stacks, tags and changes dates, descriptions and locations. Visibility archives items or marks them sensitive. Jobs refreshes thumbnails and metadata.

Most bulk actions can be reversed. After Archive, a toast says what changed and offers Undo, and the selection bar keeps an Undo too. Selections over five hundred items run on the server, and you follow them in Activity.

Compare helps you pick the best of several similar shots. Select them and choose Compare. The first one you selected stays pinned on the left, and the others come up beside it, one at a time.

Click a photo to zoom both at the same point, and move to pan them together. Choose Keep or Reject under each. Deciding on the right-hand photo brings up the next one, and Undo decision steps back.

Keep writes five stars, and Reject marks the photo as rejected, so every view and search can use the result. Choose Done, filter by Rejected, and delete the rejects together.

Filters narrow the view, the selection bar acts on it, and Compare decides.

Next up: People.

## Production notes

- Interface vs docs: searching.md does not cover the Filter control, chips, exclusion, the selection bar or Compare, and duplicates-utility.md covers only the duplicate review page (its compare view, "Keep this copy", belongs to CARE-02). All labels here are the current interface's; flag both docs.
- Chip wording on the library toolbar names the field, not the value: one person reads "People", several read "People (3)", a text or place value reads "City: Banff", and an exclusion reads "Not {field}" ("Not Tags", "Not Favorite"). There is no "Not" toggle on a chip; exclusion comes from Exclude in a group's match-mode menu, from No on a Yes / No choice, or from a leading minus in the search palette (LIB-08). The search results page uses different chip wording ("People: without Jamie", "City: not Banff"); keep this episode in library views.
- Compare exists in library views only (not on the search results page). It needs at least two selected items ("Select at least two matching items to compare."). The pinned item is always the first selected; there is no control to change the pin. Keep writes five stars and Reject writes the rejected mark; choosing the same decision again clears it. Only the owner can decide ("Only the owner can keep or reject this item."), so use Taylor's own frames. There are no on-screen keyboard hints; Esc returns to the grid.
- Undo: the result toast reads "{Action}: {summary}" with Undo for eight seconds; the bar shows "Undo: {reverse action}". Over 500 items the action runs on the server ("{action}: {n} items queued. Follow it in Activity.") and only Archive offers an undo then. Beat 7's inset uses a mocked large selection; do not archive 1,200 real items during capture.
- The selection bar's Compare, Quick edit and Open in Studio appear in library views; Quick edit and Studio are covered in the EDIT series and are not narrated. When they are present, the other actions show as icons with tooltips; CALLOUTs name them.
- Beat 10's delete moves the two rejects to Trash (restorable, LIB-03). Restore them after capture.
