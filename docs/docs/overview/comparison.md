# Is Frameleaf right for your library?

Frameleaf is for people who want to keep original media on storage they control, browse it from a web application, organize and search it, and choose how to share and process it.

You operate the server and its backups. [Manager](/install/manager) guides installation and recovery; [manual Compose](/install/docker-compose) gives administrators direct container control. Optional models and editing workers have their own hardware requirements.

Start with a small representative collection. Check your [file formats](/features/supported-formats), [search](/features/searching), [sharing](/features/sharing) and [backup recovery](/administration/backup-and-restore) before moving your only copy of a library.
