# Uploads and downloads

Frameleaf uploads and downloads through two panels that stay on screen while you keep browsing: **Uploads** and **Downloads**. They sit at the bottom corner of the window and can be minimised.

## Uploading

- **Upload** in the top bar offers files or a folder, and an album to put them in. You can also drag files onto a library page.
- Each file gets a row with its size and state: waiting, uploading with a percentage, uploaded, already in your library (a duplicate is recognised by its content and not stored twice), or the error.
- **Retry failed** uploads the failed files again. **Cancel remaining** stops the files still waiting and the ones on their way; files already uploaded stay. **Clear finished** removes the uploaded and duplicate rows while the rest continue.
- Uploads keep going while you move between pages of the app.

### If you reload or close the tab

An upload is not resumable. Reloading or closing the tab stops the files still waiting or on their way, and the panel starts empty afterwards; nothing continues in the background. Files that finished uploading are in your library. To finish the rest, upload the same files again: the ones already uploaded are recognised as duplicates and skipped.

## Downloading

- A single item downloads its current version; **Download original** gets the untouched file of an edited item.
- Several items, an album or a whole view download as zip archives. An archive bigger than the size limit in your download settings is split into several parts, each with its own row.
- Live Photos include their motion clip. The video embedded in some Android motion photos is included only when **Embedded videos** is turned on in your download settings.
- A row shows what is being prepared, then **Save**. A failed row offers **Retry**; **Cancel** stops a download that is still being prepared.
- Only what you may download is ever listed. A Locked item is never downloaded or named unless you have unlocked Locked content in this session, and a public link that does not allow downloads offers none. Locking the session or signing out clears the panel.

A prepared download is held by this tab until you save it; it does not survive a reload.
