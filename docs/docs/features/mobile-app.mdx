# Mobile App

Frameleaf has native apps for iPhone and iPad (iOS or iPadOS 26 or later) and for Android (Android 11 or later). Both back up your phone to your server, check every upload against the server's copy, and let you browse your whole library at home, away or offline.

## Free Up Space

Once your server holds an identical copy of a photo, checked by SHA-256, **Free Up Space** can remove it from the phone. You see a summary first, and removed items go to the phone's own trash or Recently Deleted, so you can still get them back.

:::info reclaim storage
To use the reclaimed space right away, empty the phone's trash or Recently Deleted folder outside of the app.
:::

Provided the server is healthy and [backed up](/administration/backup-and-restore.md), photos removed by Free Up Space can always be opened in the app.

### iCloud Photos

iCloud Photos syncs deletions: removing a photo from your iPhone also removes it from iCloud and every Apple device signed in with the same Apple Account. After using **Free Up Space**, the photo is stored **only** on your Frameleaf server (and in the phone's Recently Deleted folder for 30 days).

:::warning iCloud & Backups
If, in addition to Frameleaf, you rely on iCloud as a secondary backup (as part of your [3-2-1](https://www.backblaze.com/blog/the-3-2-1-backup-strategy/) backup strategy), use `Optimize iPhone Storage` in [iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105061) instead.
:::

### Chat apps (WhatsApp, etc.)

On Android, chat apps such as **WhatsApp** show media from files on the phone. If the app backs up those folders and you run **Free Up Space**, the images can disappear from your chats; you will only be able to view them in Frameleaf. Leave those folders out of backup, or review the summary carefully before removing anything.

## Away from home

At home the apps talk to your server directly. To reach it from anywhere without opening ports on your router, link your server to Frameleaf Cloud and turn on [remote access](/guides/remote-access). Push notifications also need the server to be linked.
