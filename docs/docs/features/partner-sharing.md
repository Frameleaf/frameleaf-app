# Partner Sharing

Sharing your library gives your partner their own copies of your photos, videos and ordinary albums. Copies appear in their library and timeline automatically. Sharing is one-way; each partner chooses separately whether to share back.

Each copy belongs to its recipient. It links to the same stored original, without uploading or analysing the media again. A library holds at most one copy of the same content, including items in its trash. External-library files and Hidden items are excluded; a Live Photo includes its Hidden motion part.

Copies include metadata, tags and faces. Photo edits and Studio develop versions are copied as snapshots. Stacks and duplicate groups use the recipient's own group records. Favourites and trash remain each person's own choices.

Descriptions, locations, capture dates, ratings, tags, faces and visibility continue to follow the source until the recipient edits that field. Album titles, descriptions, covers and membership follow the same rule. Renaming or moving a tag updates the photos that use it, including descendant tags.

Locked media arrives locked. The recipient uses their own PIN to view it. A sharing partner's PIN grants no access to another person's Locked view.

## Sharing with a Partner

1. Open **Settings > People & sharing > Partners & recipient groups**.
2. Select **Add partner**, choose the account and confirm.
3. Existing media is copied in the background; new media is copied as it arrives.

Interrupted copies resume without creating another asset. Retries complete pairing, tags, faces, album membership and onward sharing while preserving recipient overrides.

## Stopping Sharing

Select **Stop sharing** next to the partner and confirm. Copies already received stay in the recipient's library and stop following the source. Access the partner granted you is kept.

Permanently deleting a received copy is remembered: retries, another backfill and restarting the partnership do not recreate it. Restoring a trashed copy uses the normal library trash.
