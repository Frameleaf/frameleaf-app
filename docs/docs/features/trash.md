# Trash and large files

**Trash** holds the photos and videos you deleted until they are removed for good. **Large files** (Settings → Utilities) lists your largest originals so you can decide what to move to the trash. Both show only your own media, as far as your session may see it.

## What the trash shows

- Only your own items. Nobody else's trash is ever listed, whatever their role.
- Locked items only after you unlock your session with your PIN. While your session is locked they stay in the trash, uncounted and untouched by **Empty your trash**, and come back into view when you unlock.
- Nothing your privacy settings hide (suppressed people, pets and tags, and hidden sensitive detections) while your session is locked.
- External-library files that went missing. A library scan puts them in the trash and brings them back when the file is found again; they are listed with a note, and the trash does not restore or delete them.

The summary counts what is listed, the combined size of the originals, and the retention window: items are eligible for permanent deletion after the number of days set in **Trash settings**, or immediately when the trash is turned off.

## Changing the trash

Every change is reviewed first and applied exactly as reviewed:

- **Restore** (one item, the selection, or everything) puts items back where they were. Albums, favorites, archive, tags, stacks and the lock are kept; trashing never removed them.
- **Delete selected** and **Empty your trash** show how many items and which ones before anything happens, and ask you to type `DELETE` and the count. **Empty your trash** always covers your whole visible trash, whatever filters are applied.
- If anything changed between the review and the confirmation (an item restored or deleted in another tab, a new item trashed, an item locked or unlocked, your session locked), nothing is changed and you are asked to review again.
- In **Large files**, **Move selected to trash** reviews the chosen items the same way, and **Undo** restores exactly the items that moved. A partner's items are listed for context but only their owner can move them.

## Freed space

File sizes are the size of each original. They are not a promise of freed disk space:

- Permanent deletion removes items in the background. The trash page says how many permanently deleted items are still being removed.
- An original that another item still uses (for example after physical deduplication) stays on disk until its last user is removed. The review says how many of the chosen items share their original, and how much of the total stays on disk.
