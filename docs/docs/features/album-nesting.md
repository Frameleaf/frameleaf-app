# Nested albums

Albums and collections can contain other albums and collections, up to 32 levels deep. An ordinary album can hold photos and videos as well as child albums. Shared spaces remain at the top level.

Use the album directory’s **New** menu or an album’s child-album section to create a child. **Move to…** and **Edit details** let an owner choose a new parent. The parent must be an album or collection they can edit. An album cannot move into itself or one of its descendants.

The sidebar shows expandable branches. Album pages show the full breadcrumb path and their immediate children. The directory also offers a searchable flat view; an item’s parent path identifies where it belongs.

Moving an album preserves its files, memberships and sharing settings. Children do not inherit access from their parent: a person who can see a child but cannot see its parent sees the child independently.

Deleting an album or collection preserves its original media and its child albums. Direct children move to the deleted item’s parent, or to the top level when it has no parent.

These organization features are free and shared by the regular library and the planned Frameleaf Pro creator workspace.
