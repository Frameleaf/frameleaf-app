- **FL-83 timeline grouping, rows at month boundaries (deviation for the owner to accept):** the prototype justifies a whole year or "All" group as one flow (`TimelineLibrary.jsx` `justifiedRows` over `group.assets`). Production loads and lays the library out one month bucket at a time (`layoutGroupedMonth` in `layout-support.svelte.ts`), so in Years and All a row can end short where one month ends and the next begins; Months and Days match the prototype. Carrying a partial row into the next month would make each month's geometry depend on the previous month being loaded and laid out, reflow every later month when an earlier one loads (moving the scroll position), and draw one month's tiles inside another month's virtualized container. That is a rework of the timeline manager's bucket model, not a contained change, so it was left out of `codex/FL-83-timeline-grouping`. Review accepted it as non-blocking. **Jira follow-up needed:** open an FL issue for the continuous year/All flow (or the owner's explicit acceptance of the deviation) and link it from FL-83; it could not be created from these sessions because the Atlassian connector is unauthorized.
