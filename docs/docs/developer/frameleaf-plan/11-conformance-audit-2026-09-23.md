### September 24 Apple-style foundation (FL-29, FL-139, FL-37)

Packet 1 of the September 24 port (FL-29 with FL-139 and FL-37) is on `codex/FL-29-apple-foundation`, based on `master/frameleaf-implementation` @ `a64ee91455`. It is recorded here rather than in the Confluence-mirrored `FL-29-web-primitives.md` until that page is next synced. No audit gap id is closed by it: the September 23 audit predates the Apple-style layer. The prototype (`design/frameleaf/template/src/apple-style.css`, `interactions.js`, `tokens.json`, INTERACTION-REQUIREMENTS.md "Apple-style refinements") is authoritative; where the September 22 chrome disagreed, the chrome was changed.

- **Tokens** (`web/src/lib/frameleaf/tokens.css`, apple-style.css:1-58): `--fl-spring` (linear() spring), `--fl-snappy`, `--fl-duration` 380 ms; `--fl-material`, `--fl-material-edge`, `--fl-material-blur` with a solid panel, ordinary border and no blur under `prefers-contrast: more` or `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`; the Display P3 accent for both themes under `color-gamut: p3`; `--fl-ai` `#5e5ce6` with white text for the reserved AI tile; `--fl-radius-sheet` (22 px); `--fl-safe-*` safe-area insets. The font stack starts with the Apple system font and falls back to bundled Inter (the `base.css` comment now says so).
- **Baseline layer** (`base.css`): balanced h1–h3, tabular numbers, `corner-shape: squircle` under `@supports` for controls, chips, `.fl-card` and sheets (cards and sheets grow their radius 1.8× as in the prototype), the springy `scale(0.96)` button press (none under Reduce Motion), and reusable `fl-material`, `fl-material-capsule` and `fl-material-sheet` classes for later packets.
- **Squircle people photos** (`web/src/app.css`): `--fl-squircle` and the `.fl-squircle` mask are global, not scoped to `.frameleaf`, because people photos also render in the viewer information panel and the shared `UserAvatar`. PersonAvatar, FaceCrop, PetThumbnail, UserAvatar (account menu, members, activity, album stacks), rule and Locked pickers, FaceTagger placeholders and the information panel use it. A mask clips rings and outlines, so the information panel's face highlight is a squircle backing and its focus ring is on the link.
- **Motion** (`web/src/lib/frameleaf/motion.ts`): `prefersReducedMotion()` through `mediaQueryManager`; `motionFly`, `motionSlide` and `motionScale` crossfade (150 ms) under Reduce Motion; `withViewTransition(update)`; `animateFlip(container, apply)` ports `animateGridChange`. AssetTile's ad-hoc `matchMedia` is gone, and every moving Svelte transition in Frameleaf, viewer and route code is gated; a spec fails if a new ungated one appears.
- **Sheets and menus**: `Dialog` has the 22 px sheet radius (squircle where supported), a 220 ms fade with a 480 ms spring rise, and a 40% scrim with a 12 px blur that becomes a solid 67% scrim under Increase Contrast or Reduce Transparency. `Menu` opens on the 320 ms spring from its anchored corner. Both crossfade under Reduce Motion; focus return and Escape are unchanged and covered.
- **Theme colour**: `app.html` ships a static `#101416` default that the pre-paint theme script switches to `#f4f6f7` for the light theme; the root layout keeps that meta in step with the app theme instead of the OS scheme.
- **Review follow-ups**:
  - `--fl-muted` and `--fl-accent` fall below 4.5:1 on the 72% material over bright or dark photographs. Text on material may therefore use only `--fl-text`, `--fl-on-material-muted` or `--fl-on-material-accent`. The tests check those over black, over white and on the solid fallback, in both themes.
  - Headings get -0.015em tracking.
  - The press rule now carries the `.button` colour and border transitions.
  - Drag handles (face boxes, the resize grip and editor mask handles) opt out of the press scale with `fl-no-press`.
  - Menus take their transform origin from the writing direction.
  - The shared-component and album-page transitions, including `animate:flip` through `motionFlip`, are gated too.

Validation: the web unit suites for `src/lib/frameleaf`, `src/lib/components/frameleaf`, `src/lib/components/asset-viewer`, `src/lib/components/shared-components` and `src/routes` pass (248 files), with a single `svelte-check` run reporting zero errors, and ESLint and Prettier clean on the changed files. The contrast tests cover text on the frosted material composited over black and white backdrops, the solid fallbacks, the P3 accent against every surface and its text, and the AI tile. No browser or e2e run is claimed here; the lead runs e2e, and the FL-29 geometry baselines need re-capturing after the radius and dialog changes.

Open: `viewport-fit=cover` is not yet set in `app.html`, because the top bar does not pad for the notch yet (Packet 2). Whether pets get the squircle is still an owner decision; they follow the people pattern for now. The global Reduce Motion clamp in `tokens.css` still reduces descendant CSS animations to an instant change; the Dialog (which is its own `.frameleaf` scope) and Menu opt back into a crossfade.
