# Thank you, Immich

Frameleaf is built on [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich), and it would not exist without the people who made Immich what it is.

Nearly 3,000 people have contributed to Immich. Some wrote the server, the web app, the machine-learning service and the mobile apps. Others fixed small bugs, translated the interface into dozens of languages, wrote and corrected documentation, filed careful bug reports, tested releases, reviewed pull requests, designed the interface, or answered questions from strangers on GitHub and Discord. Every one of those contributions counts. Together they turned a self-hosted photo backup idea into one of the most loved open-source projects around.

## What we owe them

When we started Frameleaf, we started from years of their work:

- a fast, reliable way to upload, store and browse a lifetime of photos and videos
- face recognition, smart search and the machine-learning pipeline behind them
- metadata handling, thumbnails, video transcoding and the storage layout
- albums, sharing, partner accounts, the timeline and the map
- a database design, an API and a test suite carefully shaped over thousands of pull requests

Building any of this from scratch would have taken a small team many years. We were able to start from a strong, well-tested base because thousands of people chose to share their time and skill openly.

## Our commitment

To everyone who has ever contributed to Immich — maintainers, first-time contributors, translators, testers and community helpers alike: thank you. Frameleaf would not have been possible without your hard work.
